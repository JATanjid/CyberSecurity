Services

Licensed nursing and rehabilitative therapies, coordinated with your physician and delivered in the comfort of home.

Skilled Nursing

Registered and licensed practical nurses providing clinical care at home under your physician’s plan of care.

  • Medication management & teaching
  • Wound & post-surgical care
  • Chronic condition monitoring

Physical Therapy

Restore strength, balance and mobility after surgery, illness or a fall — without travelling to a clinic.

  • Fall prevention
  • Post-operative rehab
  • Gait & balance training

Occupational Therapy

Regain the skills for everyday living — dressing, bathing, cooking — with safe, practical strategies at home.

  • Daily living skills

  • Adaptive equipment

Speech Therapy

Getting started

Care at home in three simple steps

We coordinate with your doctor, hospital or insurance so you can focus on getting better.

Call or request online

Tell us about your needs. Our intake team answers your questions and verifies coverage.

In-home assessment

A registered nurse visits to assess needs and build a personalized plan of care with your physician.

Care begins

Your nurse and therapists visit on schedule, keep your family informed, and track your progress.

FAQ

Questions families ask us

Don’t see your question? Our intake team is happy to help.

(718) 528-5493
How do I start home care services?

Call our intake team or submit the request form. We’ll confirm eligibility, coordinate with your physician, and schedule a nurse to visit for an initial assessment.

Do I need a doctor’s order?

Skilled nursing and therapy services require a physician’s order and plan of care. If you don’t have one yet, our team can help coordinate with your doctor or hospital discharge planner.

What insurance do you accept?

Coverage depends on your plan. Please call our office with your insurance information and our intake team will verify your benefits before services begin.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve patients in Queens, Brooklyn, the Bronx, Manhattan, Staten Island and Nassau County.

How do I report a concern or compliance issue?

You can call our confidential Compliance Hotline at any time during posted hours. Reports may be made anonymously and without fear of retaliation.

Ready to bring care home?

Speak with our intake team today — we’ll answer your questions and help you get started.
