Our services

Hospital-quality care, delivered to your door

Our licensed clinicians work with your physician to help you heal, regain independence and manage your health safely at home.

Skilled Nursing

Registered and licensed practical nurses providing clinical care at home under your physician’s plan of care.

Learn more

Physical Therapy

Restore strength, balance and mobility after surgery, illness or a fall — without travelling to a clinic.

Learn more

Occupational Therapy

Regain the skills for everyday living — dressing, bathing, cooking — with safe, practical strategies at home.

Learn more

Speech Therapy

Support for communication, cognition and swallowing difficulties following stroke, injury or illness.

Learn more

Respiratory Therapy

Breathing support and education for patients managing COPD, asthma and other respiratory conditions.

Learn more

Respite Care

Short-term relief for family caregivers, so your loved one is safe and cared for while you rest and recharge.

Getting started

Care at home in three simple steps

We coordinate with your doctor, hospital or insurance so you can focus on getting better.

Call or request online

Tell us about your needs. Our intake team answers your questions and verifies coverage.

In-home assessment

A registered nurse visits to assess needs and build a personalized plan of care with your physician.

Care begins

Your nurse and therapists visit on schedule, keep your family informed, and track your progress.

Why families choose CHCS

Clinical excellence with a neighbor’s heart

  • Accredited quality. Licensed by NYS DOH and accredited by The Joint Commission for home care.
  • One coordinated team. Nurses and therapists share one plan of care and keep your doctor in the loop.
  • Rooted in Queens. A local agency serving New York families since 1993.
  • Family kept informed. Clear communication and family resources at every step.
